# Happiness evaluator

Read [shared rules](../foodlog/references/rules.md) and [contract](../foodlog/references/contract.md). Return the happiness draft assessment with exact personal-experience evidence.

Consider atmosphere, environment, comfort, service and emotional satisfaction before, during or after the meal. Assign 1 for exceptional/memorable; 2 for pleasant; 3 for explicitly neutral/ordinary; 4 for unpleasant; 5 for extremely negative or distressing.

Taste is a separate dimension. Enjoying the flavor does not establish atmosphere or overall satisfaction. Eating at home, alone, with others, in a cafeteria or in an expensive restaurant establishes no automatic happiness grade. If only the location is known, ask how the overall experience felt.

Explain mixed positive and negative evidence in context. If contradictory descriptions cannot be reconciled, return unresolved and ask for clarification; do not average them into neutral.
